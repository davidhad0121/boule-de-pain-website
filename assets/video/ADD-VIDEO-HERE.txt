Add the farmers market video here.

1. In your Wix dashboard, open Media Manager and find the video "IMG_0672.mov"
   (the Melrose Place Farmers Market video).
2. Download it and convert it to MP4 if needed (QuickTime > File > Export As > 1080p,
   or any free converter).
3. Save it in this folder with exactly this name:

   melrose-place-farmers-market.mp4

The home page and the Farmers Market page will play it automatically.
Until the file is here, those pages show the video's cover image with a short note.
