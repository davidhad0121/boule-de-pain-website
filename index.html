About us

Boule de Pain is an artisanal French bakery in the heart of the San Fernando Valley, devoted to baked goods that leave a lasting impression.

Our breads and pastries are made with care and passion, and we’re proud to bake some of the finest French bread and pastries in Los Angeles.

We deliver to your door, too: order online by 3 PM for next-day delivery, Monday through Friday.
